2D-to-3D Human Pose

Master’s thesis: pose lifting and viewpoint transformation.

Inside the project

The problem

Human behavior recognition should account for changes in camera viewpoint, rather than treating each angle as an entirely separate problem.

My contribution

As part of my master’s thesis, I extended existing pose-estimation baselines to use AlphaPose inputs, convert COCO joints to Human3.6M, estimate 3D skeletons, and transform skeleton references. Rashid Ali is credited as another contributor.

The approach

A PyTorch 2D-to-3D pipeline, Optuna hyperparameter tuning, skeleton-reference transformation, and notebooks for pose visualization. This builds on AlphaPose and the published 3D Pose Baseline implementations.

The context

Academic research on human behavior recognition inside elevators with variable camera angles. This public repository is a research artifact, not a current production system.

From research to engineering

This thesis work taught me to connect model inputs, coordinate conventions, evaluation, and visualization. Those concerns continue in my professional computer vision work, alongside application and product engineering.

Shopee Product Matching

Representation learning. 6 / 139 teams, top 5%.

Inside the project

The problem

Recognize whether two product listings describe the same item, supporting better matching across a marketplace.

Our contribution

A collaborative NDSC project with Farhan Tandia, Mahdin Rohmatillah, and Martin Dominikus. I contributed to the team’s product-matching experiments.

The approach

The README documents Siamese networks, contrastive loss, EfficientNetB3, augmentation, and optimizer experiments. The checked-in competition notebook uses a shared EfficientNetB6 with pairwise features and binary cross-entropy: these are different experiments.

Optimization & algorithms

Traveling Salesman Problem

Genetic algorithms, selection, crossover, and mutation.

Inside the project

The problem

Visit every city and return to the start while minimizing the total route distance.

My contribution

I implemented and explored a genetic algorithm for an Evolutionary Computation course project, with configurable populations, generations, and route visualizations.

The approach

Candidate routes compete through binary tournament selection. Crossover and mutation repair repeated cities; fitness evaluation and truncation selection form subsequent populations.

The context

An academic optimization experiment. Genetic algorithms search for useful solutions, but this project does not establish a guaranteed global optimum or a production routing service.

Readmory

A second home for what you read.

Books, highlights, and reading history in one local-first workspace. Built for Kobo, KOReader, and Kobalt.

Open Readmory
Inside the project

The problem

Reading records live across devices, databases, and annotation files. I wanted a place to connect books, highlights, and personal reflections.

What I built

A browser-based workspace that imports reader databases, keeps a local library, connects ideas across books, and exports notes and backups.

The engineering

Reader-specific imports preserve source identity. Repeated imports update existing records. Timed sessions, inferred intervals, and aggregate counters remain distinct.

The tradeoff

Local-first storage avoids a server library, but browser data can be cleared. Backups matter. Optional metadata enrichment is disclosed separately.

A regional trip planner connecting a searchable places library, maps, and source-grounded AI suggestions.

Inside the project

The idea

Turn a trip brief into a useful starting point, with places a person can inspect on a map rather than an itinerary with no sources.

What I built

A Skeleton-based View-Invariant Framework for Human Fall Detection in an Elevator

2022 IEEE International Conference on Industrial Technology (ICIT)

Inside the project

The problem

Elevator cameras see people from different angles, while emergency fall examples are difficult to collect.

The approach

The published framework combines AlphaPose 2D keypoints, 3D pose estimation, Procrustes alignment to a reference camera, and a fall-recognition model.

My contribution

Co-authored with Rashid Ali, Lan-Da Van, and Yu-Chee Tseng. The related public thesis repository contains my pose-conversion, tuning, and visualization work.

The outcome

Published at ICIT 2022. The work studies transferring recognition across camera views; the case study does not claim a deployed safety certification.
